# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and when
you represent the project elsewhere.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. The full text of the pledge, the standards and the
enforcement guidelines is the Contributor Covenant's, at the link above.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers at
[opensource@integraledger.com](mailto:opensource@integraledger.com). Every report is reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

This address is for conduct only. Report a vulnerability through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on this
repository, as [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) says, never by email.
